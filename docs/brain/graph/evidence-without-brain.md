# Brain's evidence tells what would have gone wrong without it

The Evidence tab in brain's window answers "does your RAG work?" with a story, not a dashboard. Each line says what would have happened without a part of brain: without brain, your agent would have gone off the rails X times and you corrected it; without brain's validator, X of Y builds would have shipped with problems; without brain's graph, your agents would have started from zero X times; without the readiness check, X briefs would have gone out with questions only you could answer; without brain's guards, agents would have done X things they must not. Every number counts concrete caught events from the usage log, and each line carries its cases as proof. The cost in estimated tokens closes the story. There is no time-saved figure and no model-written text.

## Why
When someone asks whether brain works, the user wants to answer "yes it does, it's a RAG over time, and these are the benefits", and present "How brain made my work with agents better". Numbers alone, and headlines with click-through, do not make people understand what brain has done for them; a story does. Framing it as "brain remembered for you" felt condescending, so each line names what brain prevented. A count of real caught cases survives the question "how do you know?", where an estimate would not.
